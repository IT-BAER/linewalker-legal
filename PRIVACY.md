# Linewalker privacy policy

_Last updated: 22 October 2026_

Linewalker is a drawing game for Android by Bruno Miglar (IT-BAER). This policy explains what happens with data when you play it.

## What the game stores

Linewalker stores your game progress on your device: stars, upgrades, your best Endless distance, your settings and whether you finished the tutorial. This data stays in the app's private storage. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this progress in your backup. You can delete it at any time with Settings > Reset progress in the game, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

## What the game does not do

Linewalker has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts. It does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers, and it does not send your game data to IT-BAER or anyone else.

## Purchases

One in-app purchase unlocks Worlds 2 to 4 and all future worlds. Google Play handles the payment and the purchase record under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). IT-BAER does not receive your payment details. The game asks Google Play whether the purchase exists and keeps only a note on your device that the full game is unlocked.

## Children

Linewalker does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases go through Google Play, where parents can require approval.
